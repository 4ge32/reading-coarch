A careful review

A review can follow every step without checking whether the original requirement still applies. The team wanted to test that difference, so they wrote down both the questions they asked and the answers the evidence actually supported.

The old devices were no longer common, but that did not prove that nobody used them. An engineer checked a second source before approving the change. This was a useful check because an incomplete report could still contain accurate observations.

Keep the uncertainty

A test may pass without showing that the implementation meets every requirement. That sentence does not say the implementation must be wrong. It says that the test alone does not establish the stronger conclusion.

The evidence is limited, but the conclusion may still be correct.

  • Keep possibility separate from certainty.
